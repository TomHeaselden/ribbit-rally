RibbitRally!

Eight hoppy frogs on jetskis. 83 splashy pond courses across 4 levels. One finish line.

How to play

SteerArrow keys or A / D. On a phone or tablet, use the big ◀ ▶ buttons.
Go & brakeYour jetski drives itself with Auto-Go on. Hold ▼ or S to brake. Turn Auto-Go off to press ▲ or W yourself.
Grab boxesDrive through the spinning ? boxes to get a surprise item.
Use itemsPress Space (or the ITEM button) to use what you picked up.
Four levelsLevel 1 is gentle. Level 2 adds sharp turns and a garden sprinkler. Level 3 is hairpin madness with swarms of insects. Hit an insect or get caught in a sprinkler's jet of water and you'll spin out! Sprinklers spray one way at a time, so go round the side the water isn't on.
Level 4 wavesSurf faster riding down a wave and slow down pushing up one. Hit the top of a wave fast and you'll fly through the air!
Watch out for the carp!Colourful koi swim around the pond. When one pops its head up with its mouth open, steer clear, or it will gulp you down and spit you high into the air!
Look out above!Birds fly over and drop poo. Watch for the growing shadow and steer away, or you'll skid round and round in the splat!
Gears & turboYour jetski has 3 gears that change by themselves as you speed up. Listen for the turbo whistle and the PSSHH of the dump valve on every gear change!
